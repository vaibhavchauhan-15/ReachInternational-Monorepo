# Ingested Business & Operational Context

**Analysis Date:** 2026-09-09

## Executive Context

ReachInternational operates in the heavy equipment, machine rental, and industrial field-service sector. The enterprise manages fleets of excavators, cranes, loaders, and specialized machinery deployed across industrial client sites.

Before this platform, machine service due dates and operator logs were tracked manually via Excel spreadsheets, leading to missed maintenance intervals, reactive breakdowns, and lack of operational transparency.

The platform transforms this operation by:
1. Centralizing machine telemetry, service histories, and rental agreements.
2. Automating morning reminder alerts via WhatsApp, SMS, and email so field engineers and admins never miss scheduled maintenance.
3. Providing mobile-first digital hour logging and breakdown tracking for field operators.
4. Enabling executive visibility across fleet utilization, machine health, and service revenue.

## Key Stakeholder Roles

1. **Super Admin / Admin**: Full organizational authority, machine fleet configuration, user account approvals, and audit log inspection.
2. **Service Manager**: Manages machine maintenance schedules, technician dispatches, breakdown tickets, and service sign-offs.
3. **Field Service Engineer & Mechanic**: Field workers who inspect machinery, log completed services, upload photo evidence, and perform repairs.
4. **Heavy Equipment Operator**: Field operators logging daily machine hour meters, overtime, and breakdown incidents.
5. **Supervisor**: Site leads overseeing equipment shifts, operator assignments, and running logs.
6. **Rental / Sales Manager**: Manages client rental contracts, delivery challans, and customer CRM pipelines.
7. **Client**: Machinery owners and industrial site managers viewing machine statuses, service histories, and reports.

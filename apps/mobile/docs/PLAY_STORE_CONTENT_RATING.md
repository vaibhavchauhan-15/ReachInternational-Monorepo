# Google Play Console — Content Rating Questionnaire Guide (IARC)

Use this guide to complete the **Content Rating Questionnaire** in Google Play Console for **Reach International** (`com.reachinternational.app`).

---

## 1. Category Selection
- **Email Address**: Enter your official developer email (e.g. `info@reachinternational.co.in` or admin email).
- **Category**: Select **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other**.

---

## 2. Questionnaire Responses

### Violence
- **Does the app contain any violent material?**
  - **NO**

### Sexuality
- **Does the app contain any sexual material or nudity?**
  - **NO**

### Language / Profanity
- **Does the app contain potentially offensive language?**
  - **NO**

### Controlled Substances
- **Does the app contain references to or depictions of illegal drugs, alcohol, or tobacco?**
  - **NO**

### Promotion of Restricted Items
- **Does the app natively allow users to buy, sell, or trade firearms, tobacco, or alcohol?**
  - **NO**

### Miscellaneous
- **Does the app natively allow users to interact or exchange content with other users through voice, text, or photo sharing?**
  - **NO** (This is an enterprise machinery telemetry and shift logging tool, not a public social networking or open messaging platform).
- **Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with other users?**
  - **NO**
- **Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods or services?**
  - **NO**
- **Does the app contain any web browser or open web search capability?**
  - **NO**
- **Is the app primarily a news or educational app?**
  - **NO**

---

## 3. Expected Result

Upon submitting the questionnaire, IARC will generate the following ratings:
- **Google Play (Global)**: **Everyone** / **3+**
- **PEGI**: **PEGI 3**
- **USK**: **USK 0**
- **ESRB**: **Everyone**
- **ClassInd**: **L (Livre)**

This confirms the app is classified as a clean enterprise utility with zero content restrictions.
